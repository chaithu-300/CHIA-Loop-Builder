# Plenary Talk: Architecture & Systems in the Era of Agentic Co-Design
**Speaker:** Dimitrios Skarlatos (Assistant Professor, Computer Science Department, Carnegie Mellon University)
**Venue:** Architecture 2.0 Workshop, ISCA 2026

## Abstract
The AI datacenter stack runs on hardware-software contracts written for a world where humans wrote all the code and designed all the hardware, but both are now changing. With datacenters projected to draw over 1,000 TWh annually and the next efficiency leap demanding two orders of magnitude of improvement, renegotiating the hardware-software contract is no longer optional.

This talk turns head-on to agentic co-design across the computing stack:
* **Embedded Learning**: Moving from static low-level logic to embedding learning inside low-level mechanisms with frameworks like LVM.
* **Operating System Re-architecting**: Rethinking the OS contract over GPUs for AI and agentic serving with systems like LithOS.
* **Full Agentic Co-Design**: Driving chiplet and macro-architecture exploration autonomously with **Agentic Architect**, an LLM-driven architecture design space exploration and optimization framework.

Architects are uniquely positioned to close the agentic loop. The benchmarks, simulators, and metrics are already here. This presentation covers what is currently missing and presents a call to action for the systems community to define the next generation of the system stack.
