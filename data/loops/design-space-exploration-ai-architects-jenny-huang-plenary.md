# Plenary Talk: Design Space Exploration in the Age of AI Architects
**Speaker:** Qijing Jenny Huang (Senior Research Scientist, NVIDIA)
**Venue:** Architecture 2.0 Workshop, ISCA 2026

## Abstract
Design space exploration (DSE) is central to computer architecture, helping designers navigate large spaces of algorithmic, architectural, and mapping choices to optimize performance, power, and area (PPA). Yet current DSE flows still require substantial manual effort and remain largely bounded by human-defined abstractions, performance models, and search spaces.

As the field approaches its "AlphaZero moment," this talk looks beyond using AI to accelerate search within a fixed design space. The larger opportunity is to build **AI architects**: agentic systems that:
1. Automate under-formalized design tasks.
2. Perform knowledge-guided searches across multiple layers.
3. Expand exploration beyond choices explicitly specified by humans.

### Highlighted Framework: SOLAR
To ground this vision, the presentation introduces **SOLAR**, an agentic framework that interprets algorithms and automatically translates them into performance-model-ready representations. This showcases how AI architects can support the less structured, early stages of DSE.

### Cross-Domain Transferability
The talk connects AI for architecture to the broader AI-for-X landscape, especially AI for coding:
* Transferable lessons from existing software coding agents.
* Hard limits and challenges unique to physical hardware design.
* The critical need for new benchmarks, evaluation gymnasiums, and infrastructure to build creative yet trustworthy AI architects for scalable DSE.
